Illustrated T-Shirt Design Services

I create illustrated T-shirt designs for brands, events, businesses, tourism products, communities, and personal projects. My work combines illustration, character design, and graphic composition to create wearable visuals that are expressive, memorable, and suitable for printing on apparel.

Illustrated T-shirts can help brands and organizations create stronger visual identity while also turning clothing into merchandise, souvenirs, promotional items, or collectible designs.

My illustration approach ranges from playful chibi-style artwork to themed character illustrations, doodle graphics, and stylized visual concepts inspired by local culture, tourism, and anime aesthetics.

This Langkawi-themed T-shirt was created as a personal project with the intention of producing wearable souvenir merchandise for tourist apparel shops in Langkawi.

The illustration was drawn in a playful chibi style to create a fun and approachable visual identity suitable for tourism-related products. The project explored how stylized character illustration can make localthemed merchandise feel more memorable and appealing to visitors.

This project also reflects my interest in creating illustrated apparel designs connected to local tourism and regional identity.

Tioman Island Trip T-shirts

This illustrated T-shirt design was created for a company involved in island-hopping services around Tioman Island.

The apparel was designed to be distributed to employees and clients as part of the company’s branding and trip-related activities. The goal of the design was to create a visual identity that captures the atmosphere of island travel, leisure, and tropical adventure.

Projects like this demonstrate how custom illustrated apparel can help businesses create stronger brand recognition while also serving as memorable event or trip merchandise.

Mamak Cat & Yakuza Cat T-shirts

These cat-themed graphic T-shirt illustrations were created for an individual client for personal use.

One of the designs features a cat holding a bowl of nasi lemak while wearing batik-inspired clothing resembling the attire commonly associated with Malaysian mamak restaurant workers. The illustration was intended to combine humor, local food culture, and character-based visual storytelling.

Another design features a stylized cat character inspired by yakuza aesthetics, including tattoo-inspired shoulder artwork and a tougher visual personality. The goal of the piece was to create a bold and playful graphic character suitable for statement apparel.

These projects reflect my interest in combining humor, character illustration, and cultural elements into wearable graphic designs.
