Illustrated Infographic Design Services

I create illustrated infographics that combine visual storytelling, educational information, and custom illustrations to make complex topics easier to understand and more engaging to read.

Illustrated infographics are useful for education, learning materials, awareness campaigns, websites, presentations, social media content, and printed publications. By combining diagrams, typography, icons, and hand-drawn illustrations, information can become more visually memorable and easier to absorb.

Rabbit VS Hare Illustrated Infographic

“Rabbit VS Hare” was my first attempt at creating an illustrated infographic focused on educational content and visual learning.

The infographic explains the differences between rabbits and hares through illustrated comparisons involving:

  • Physical features
  • Habitat differences
  • Behavioral traits
  • Diet and food sources

The goal of the project was to explore how illustration can make educational information more engaging and easier to understand compared to plain text alone.

This project also represents my interest in entering the educational illustration and learning material industry through visually driven informational design.

Educational and Informational Illustration Approach

I enjoy turning informational topics into visuals that feel more lively, friendly, and accessible. Instead of presenting information in a purely technical format, I aim to use illustration to create curiosity and improve visual engagement.

Depending on the project, infographic illustrations may include:

  • Character illustrations
  • Simplified educational diagrams
  • Decorative doodles
  • Visual comparison layouts
  • Labelled illustrations
  • Step-by-step educational visuals
  • Animal and object illustrations
  • Icon-based information graphics

I am particularly interested in projects that involve combining learning with creative illustration styles.
