Illustrated Packaging Design Services

I create illustrated packaging designs for brands, products, gifts, food businesses, fashion products, and specialty merchandise. My illustration style ranges from clean black and white doodles to decorative themed illustrations inspired by local culture, lifestyle, and storytelling.

This illustrated packaging concept was created for a hijab brand called “Inani.” The project uses a black and white doodle illustration style inspired by everyday Muslim women’s activities during the month of Ramadhan.

The doodles feature objects and moments commonly associated with the sacred month, including modest fashion, food, prayer-related elements, and daily routines. The goal of the packaging is to create a warm and relatable visual identity that portrays how the product accompanies Muslim women throughout Ramadhan.

This concept was also influenced by packaging approaches used by popular Malaysian hijab brands that incorporate illustrated storytelling as part of limited or special edition product releases.

Illustrated packaging can help products feel more memorable, personal, and emotionally connected to customers. Instead of relying only on typography or generic stock graphics, custom illustrations allow brands to communicate identity, atmosphere, and values through visual storytelling.

This packaging concept was designed for “Rizzara Kek Lapis Sarawak,” featuring a soft pastry and milky color theme combined with decorative doodle illustrations.

The illustrations focus on Sarawak’s tourism identity by incorporating iconic local landmarks, tourist attractions, and traditional handicraft elements into the packaging visuals. The purpose of the design is not only to decorate the product, but also to communicate appreciation for Sarawak’s culture and tourism.

By combining playful illustration with regional identity, the packaging becomes more memorable to both tourists and local customers.
